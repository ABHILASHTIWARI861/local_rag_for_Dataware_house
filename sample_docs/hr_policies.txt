HALDEN LOGISTICS - HR POLICIES & WORKING HOURS (SUMMARY)

1. WORKING HOURS & BREAK SCHEDULES
- Standard full-time shifts are 8 hours per day, including one 30-minute unpaid lunch break and two 15-minute paid rest breaks.
- Rest breaks cannot be combined or taken at the beginning or end of a shift to leave early.
- Overtime must be pre-approved in writing by the Warehouse Operations Director.

2. SICKNESS & ABSENCE REPORTING
- If an employee is unable to attend their shift due to illness, they must call the Shift Hotline (+44 20 7946 0912) at least 2 hours before shift start.
- Text messages, WhatsApp, and emails are not accepted as valid notification of absence.
- A doctor's medical certificate is required for any continuous absence exceeding 3 calendar days.

3. ANNUAL LEAVE ENTITLEMENT
- Warehouse operators are entitled to 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year plus statutory public holidays.
- Peak holiday embargo periods apply during Black Friday week and the last two weeks of December; leave requests during these weeks require Operations VP approval.
