HALDEN LOGISTICS - EQUIPMENT RUNBOOK: FORKLIFT FLEET (E-SERIES 2000)

1. DAILY PRE-OPERATION INSPECTION
Before starting any shift, the operator must complete the 5-point inspection:
- Hydraulic fluid level must be within the crosshatched region on the dipstick.
- Mast chains and lift cables must show no visible signs of slack or fraying.
- Tire pressure for pneumatic models must be 6.5 bar (95 PSI).
- Brake pedal travel must engage within the first 25mm of downward pressure.
- Emergency power cutoff switch must be tested by pressing and resetting.

2. BATTERY CHARGING PROTOCOL
- Electric forklifts must only be charged in the ventilated Charging Bay (Zone B).
- Do not opportunity-charge lithium-ion battery packs if temperature exceeds 45°C.
- Lead-acid models require topping up with demineralized water every Friday afternoon.
- Charging cables must be hung on wall brackets when not connected to avoid run-over damage.

3. REPORTING BREAKDOWNS
If any fault warning code appears on the digital dash (e.g. Error Code E-402: Inverter Overheat):
- Park the forklift safely outside pedestrian walkways.
- Lower the forks fully to the ground and set the parking brake.
- Place the yellow "OUT OF SERVICE" tag on the steering wheel.
- Report the issue immediately to Maintenance Lead at extension 408.
