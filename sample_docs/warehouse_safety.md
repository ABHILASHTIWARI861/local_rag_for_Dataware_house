# Halden Logistics - Warehouse Safety Procedures

## 1. Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
All personnel entering the warehouse floor must wear:
- Steel-toe safety boots (EN ISO 20345 certified).
- High-visibility reflective vest (Class 2 minimum).
- Hard hat when working in high-bay racking aisles (Zones C and D).
- Protective gloves when handling pallet strapping or wooden crates.

## 2. Forklift and Pedestrian Safety
- The maximum speed limit for forklifts inside the warehouse is 8 km/h.
- Pedestrians must use designated green painted walkways at all times.
- Forklift operators must sound the horn at all blind corners and aisle intersections.
- A minimum safety distance of 3 meters must be maintained between moving equipment and pedestrians.

## 3. Emergency Evacuation and Fire Procedures
- Fire exits are marked with illuminated green signs and must never be obstructed.
- In case of fire alarm, stop all machinery immediately, leave the building via the nearest fire exit, and assemble at Assembly Point 2 (North Car Park).
- Shift supervisors are responsible for roll call using the daily visitor and employee register.
