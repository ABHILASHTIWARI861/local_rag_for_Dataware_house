# Halden Logistics - Shift Handover Rules

## 1. Handover Timing
- The outgoing shift supervisor and incoming shift supervisor must meet 15 minutes prior to the start of the next shift (e.g., 14:45 for the 15:00 afternoon shift).
- Both supervisors must sign off on the Physical Handover Log located in Dispatch Office 1.

## 2. Mandatory Information Exchange
During handover, the following details must be communicated:
1. Pending orders and high-priority dispatches scheduled for the next 4 hours.
2. Grounded or damaged machinery (including equipment tag numbers).
3. Any safety near-misses or incidents recorded during the shift.
4. Staff attendance, absenteeism, and temporary agency coverage counts.

## 3. Discrepancy Reconciliation
If physical inventory counts do not match the WMS (Warehouse Management System) end-of-shift batch report:
- Do not sign off the log until the Shift Manager on duty has been notified.
- Any discrepancy exceeding €500 requires an automated incident flag in the inventory portal.
